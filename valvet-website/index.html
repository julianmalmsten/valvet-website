02 / ABOUT THE BAND

Music for the day
you never forget.

Valvet is an alternative rock band from Malmö, Sweden, whose music thrives on contrasts: euphoria and despair, discovery and nostalgia.

At its core lies an emotional intensity, expressed through massive guitars, storming drums, striking melodies and cinematic soundscapes that shift between grandeur and vulnerability.

Driven by the joy of playing together, Valvet brings this emotional world to life on stage, balancing raw intensity with a genuine sense of connection.
